The platform positions itself as a "unique product with no analogues on the market" — result of "9 months of development by 17 specialists." It offers casino + sports betting verticals with a commission-based revenue share model.

  • Deposit Bonus — Matching deposits (e.g., 50% bonus on $100)
  • AutoPush — Automated promo windows with "Claim Bonus" / "Make Deposit" buttons

Deposit Extraction: Cascading Withdrawal Blocks

  1. $100 — Account Verification
    Withdrawals blocked: "Available only for verified accounts." Must deposit exactly $100. If $95 deposited, verification refused.
  2. $300 — Payment Method Verification
    "KYC/AML compliance" requires $300 from the same payment source intended for withdrawal.
  3. VIP Status Block
    Account lacks "VIP Silver" status. Users accumulate "XP" through deposits. Framed as "unlocking privileges."
  4. 30-Day Waiting Period
    New accounts get manufactured restriction. Bypassed through VIP upgrades (more deposits).
  5. Security Score
    Must reach "100/100" through additional deposits.
  6. AML Check
    "High-risk activity from multiple IPs" — demands deposits to reduce risk rating.
  7. Regional Restrictions
    Alleges legal prohibitions, offers bypass for 10% fee.
  8. Tax/Duty Payments
    Fabricated financial obligations before processing.

Communication Tips

Scripts include: initial rapport building ("How may I address you?"), clarification requests for "account data (ID, full name)," escalation phrases ("I'll connect a specialist"), deposit delay excuses ("up to 5 minutes"), and account block explanations. Emphasizes "client psychology" understanding.

4. Panel Navigation

Domain Management

Create domains, auto-generated nameserver records, DNS config, Google Search Console integration via TXT record. Propagation: 1-2 hours. Indexing: ~12 hours.

Promo Codes

Configurable: bonus amount (USD), wagering conditions, bonus type (Standard / Free Bet), expiration. Wagering: total bets must equal promo amount. Free Bet at 2.0 odds: $500 bet returning $1,000 credits only $500 net.

Account Config

Crypto wallet connections required (platform cannot operate without). Settings → Security → Wallets. 2FA via TOTP (Google Authenticator, Authy). Each account gets individual wallet address. Unsupported tokens handled manually.
