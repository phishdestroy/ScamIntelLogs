Overview

TrustWalletPanel (internally tttadmin.com) is a sophisticated, custom-built phishing operation impersonating Trust Wallet. Operating since January 2025 across 9+ rotating domains and 8+ IP addresses, it uses a live chat system where Russian-speaking operators manually social-engineer victims into sending cryptocurrency deposits under the pretense of “asset replacement,” “OFAC compliance,” and “wallet unblocking.”

Over 14 months of operation, the panel has processed 1,900 victim sessions with 679 active victims. At least $100K+ stolen based on victim statements in chat. The real total is impossible to fully calculate — most transfers go through TRON (TRC-20 USDT), and the scammers rotate wallets.

Scam Flow

1. Discovery

Victim finds phishing domain via Telegram channels, romance scams (pig-butchering), or search results.

2. Fake Wallet

Phishing site mimics Trust Wallet multi-chain interface with functional swap/staking UI. Victim creates or imports wallet — mnemonic seed phrase and password captured.

3. Live Chat Trap

When victim tries to withdraw, the transaction “fails.” Live chat activates — operator impersonates Trust Wallet Support, claims assets frozen due to OFAC/AML violations.

4. Repeat Extraction

Victim told to deposit for “asset replacement.” If they pay, operator demands more. If they refuse — threatened with “OFAC jurisdiction transfer” with tight deadlines.

Financial Damage — $100K+

The total damage is impossible to fully calculate. Scammers rotate wallets, use TRON (TRC-20 USDT) as the primary channel, and victims don't always report exact amounts. Based on victim statements in chat, the minimum confirmed damage exceeds $100,000. The real number is likely much higher.
