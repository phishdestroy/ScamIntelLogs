How the Scam Works

1. Social Media Ad

Instagram/TikTok/Discord ads: "Exclusive celebrity partnership. Get $2,500 free credit with promo code!"

2. Rigged Wins

Victim plays rigged slots. Engine shows fake wins via "automatic spin-up." Balance grows. Wants to withdraw.

3. Verification Trap

"Deposit ~$100 to verify identity." Fake ToS with %sum_verif% variables. Withdrawal reminder popup every 3 hours.

4. AI Re-Deposit

GAMBLER AI "closes objections" 24/7. 80% repeat deposit rate. Victim keeps depositing for weeks/months.

5. Wallet Drain

Final: "Connect wallet to withdraw." WalletConnect drains all crypto. Record: $90K single drain.
